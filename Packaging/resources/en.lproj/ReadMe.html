How to Use · FH-KAOK

Open it the way you would a real component stereo.

Tuner

Browse cities and frequencies, then light TUNED / STEREO. Stations from around the world settle in through the amber needle.

Local music

Press EJECT and load your MP3s — the cassette deck remembers which reel you were on.

Tone

GRAPHIC EQUALIZER, MEGA BASS, LOUDNESS — nudge by feel, then turn VOLUME until it just covers the night.

Power

POWER OFF on the right is the master switch. SLEEP sets a countdown; when the song fades, the lights go with it.

Menus

Use Settings → Language for English / 中文 / Français / Português. About and Help follow the same language.
