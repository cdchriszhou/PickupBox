How to Use · Pickup Box

A player that lets you see the music.

Tuner
Browse cities and frequencies, then light TUNED / STEREO.

Local music
Press EJECT and load your MP3s — cassette or phono deck follows along.

Tone
GRAPHIC EQUALIZER, MEGA BASS, LOUDNESS — nudge by feel.

Power
POWER OFF is the master switch. SLEEP sets a countdown.

Menus
Settings → Language · Fascia for panel color themes.
